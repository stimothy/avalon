# One Act per test

A test has three parts: setup, one Act, and assertions. All setup comes before the Act. All assertions come after it.

- Fixtures and parametrization count as setup.
- A `pytest.raises` block that wraps the Act counts as the Act and its assertion.

The law-reviewer reads the tests that a change touches and reports a test with more than one Act.
