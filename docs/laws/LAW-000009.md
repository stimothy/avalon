# Writing standard

This is the standard that LAW-000009 requires every doc to follow. It is a draft until this repo's round table ratifies it.
Agents that write or edit docs load it. Other sessions do not.

## Content

- A document holds only what is true when it is read. Replaced content is removed, not kept beside its replacement.
- Every line is true on its own. A line that states something untrue, or never chosen, carries its marker on the same line.
- Each fact has one home. Other documents link to it and do not restate it.
- Unknown is written as unknown. A document never invents a rationale, a history, or an intent.
- A document has one subject. The title names it and the summary states it.
- Material goes into the document that owns its subject. A document is created only when no document owns the subject.
- A document is split when it covers more than one subject or passes the size cap. A split leaves no stub, and the links and the index are updated.

## Prose

- A sentence holds one idea. Parallel items become a list, and comparisons become a table.
- A paragraph holds one point and breaks where a reader would expect. It has no length cap.
- An edit is woven in, not appended. The writer rewrites the paragraph, splits it, or adds one, so that the passage reads as if it were written in one sitting.
- Documents use the present tense. A word that places something in time, such as "previously", "new", or "now", appears only in a changelog.
- A reader who has never seen the repo can follow each document. A term is defined once, in the glossary, and linked after that.

## Diagrams

- ADRs and design docs may hold diagrams, written in Mermaid in a fenced block.
- A sentence before the diagram says what it shows.
- The diagram is the home for what connects to what. The prose says why, and what each part does.
- A diagram shows only the present. It never draws a rejected alternative or an earlier design.
- A diagram has at most fifteen nodes. A larger one is split, or its document is.
- The allowed types are flowchart, sequence, state, and entity-relationship.

## Images

- Design docs may hold images. ADRs may not.
- An image is used only for what Mermaid cannot draw, such as a screenshot, a mock-up, a map, a sketch, or artwork.
- Images live in `docs/design/assets/`. A file is named for its document and its subject, such as `overview-site-map.png`.
- Every image has alt text that says what it shows, and never carries a fact that the text lacks.
- An image is at most 500 KB, in PNG, JPEG, SVG, or WebP.
